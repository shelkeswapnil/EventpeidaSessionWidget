Advancing the Future of the Appraisal Profession

In a profession defined by precision, credibility, and adaptability, staying ahead requires forward thinking and a willingness to evolve. The 2026 ASA International Conference (ASAIC26) invites you to explore A New Frontier: Appraising in a World of Change — a dynamic program designed to equip valuation professionals with the tools, insights, and perspective needed to lead through transformation.

Experience engaging sessions led by respected industry leaders and global thought partners who bring innovative strategies, emerging trends, and practical solutions to today’s most pressing challenges. From technological advancements and AI integration to shifting regulatory environments and global market volatility, ASAIC26 will help you navigate change with confidence.

With more than 70 educational sessions across six specialized tracks, ASAIC26 offers unparalleled depth and diversity of learning. Whether you attend in person in Fort Worth, Texas, or join virtually via live stream, you’ll gain actionable knowledge that strengthens your expertise and expands your professional network. In additional all registered attendees will have OnDemand access to session recordings for up to 90 days following the conference.

We can't wait to see you in Fort Worth as we chart the next chapter of the appraisal profession — together (y'all).
